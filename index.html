What you are seeing. The fly walks at a base speed of 8 mm/s because the network is silent without input. When a wall comes within 10 mm along an eye's rays, that eye's looming detectors receive Poisson spikes scaled by proximity; the connectome routes this to the turning DNs of the opposite side, and the fly turns away. The randomness in the path comes from those Poisson inputs, not from the wiring. Letters are assigned to the 3 × 9 grid by a seeded permutation; a key is typed only when the fly enters its region.
