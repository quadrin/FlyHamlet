01 Can it keep going by itself?

Ready for a new experiment

The phrase lab needed six stored responses to recite “to be or not to be”. Here nothing is stored. The network runs without reset, one snapshot of its voltages and currents is decoded into a letter, and that letter is presented back to it as the next cue. Whatever it remembers about where it is in the phrase has to live in its own state.

Self-driven recall, with an explicit model hypothesis. The slower condition lengthens neural time constants and rescales synaptic weights; it is an engineering experiment, not measured fruit-fly physiology. The readout that turns state into letters is external and supervised. Reading internal voltages is more permissive than the spike-only Memory lab.

03 Protocol & scientific limits+

Task. The reference is “to be or not to be”, 18 characters over the fixed seven-symbol alphabet plus END. Each cue drives its sensory partition at 100 Hz for 100 ms, followed by a 25 ms gap with no input. One snapshot is read at the end of the gap and the next cue begins immediately. In the two retained conditions one network runs through the whole episode without reset. Every episode starts from rest with its own noise seed; episode seeds are shared across conditions.

What is measured. Each snapshot reads the current membrane voltage relative to rest and the synaptic current of every nonstimulated neuron, rounded to the nearest 0.01 mV, and reports 128 pool means of each. All annotated eye cells are excluded. No earlier snapshot, cue label, clock, step index or previous output is a feature; only the current state is.

What learns. Six teacher-forced training episodes present START followed by the phrase and label each snapshot with the next character or END. One scaler with training-only statistics and one eight-way ridge readout are fitted per condition, then frozen. Three further teacher-forced episodes with fresh noise measure next-letter accuracy without compounding errors. Six recall episodes then start from START alone: the readout’s letter becomes the next cue, END stops the episode, and a fixed cap of 32 decisions does not depend on the phrase length.

Slower dynamics. The hypothesis condition multiplies the membrane and synaptic time constants by 10 and every signed synaptic weight by 0.1, so each synaptic event keeps its integrated voltage response while its peak falls. Delay, refractory period and thresholds are unchanged. These parameters are imposed, not identified from fruit-fly memory data. The reset control uses the same slower model and identical cues, then replaces all neural state at every cue onset, so each of its snapshots depends only on the current letter.

Interpretation. Chain length is the longest correct prefix of the output. A decoder with no memory at all, fitted on the one-hot current cue and rolled out on its own feedback, produces “to be be be…” and reaches 6; a chain that stops near there has not used any memory. Success under slower dynamics shows that the imposed model can carry sequence position in its state and that an external readout can read it; it does not show that the connectome learned, that flies remember phrases, or anything about the full play. Failure under original dynamics characterizes this model, stimulation and readout.

Reproducibility. Export the complete protocol, both neural parameter sets, every snapshot’s measurements, scalers, frozen readouts, predictions, cues, seeds and graph hashes. All errors are retained. New loads default to 1×; actual simulation speed depends on the device. The first run downloads about 49 MB and then prepares the rescaled comparison network.
